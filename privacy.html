Privacy, explained.

Your content is processed on your iPhone. No app account, advertising or developer analytics.

Privacy policy · Updated 3 November 2026

The essentials

We do not receive your documents for processing. Files stay on your iPhone or in storage you choose. If you email us or share a support report, we receive the information you send. Website hosting and your chosen storage services have their own privacy practices.

Who we are

Tally is made by River Investment Partners LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in England and Wales (number OC457091), registered office Flat 23, Russell Chambers, London WC1A 2JU. You can contact us at support@riverinvest.co.uk.

What Tally collects

Tally has no app account system, advertising, tracking or developer-run analytics. Your documents are not uploaded to us for processing. Optional support emails, shared reports and storage services you choose are described below.

How your receipts are read

When you scan or import a receipt, the text is read on your iPhone using Apple's built-in text recognition and, where available, Apple Intelligence running on the device. The receipt picture and its details are not uploaded for reading.

Where your receipts are kept

Receipts are saved as picture files in places you choose:

  • on your iPhone, in Tally's own folder (shown in the Files app), or
  • in your iCloud Drive, in Tally's folder or in folders you pick for each property.

Files in iCloud Drive are stored by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple's own privacy policy. We have no access to them. If you share a folder with someone else, they can see the receipts in it.

Settings

Your Tally settings, such as your name, property names and rules, are kept on your iPhone and synced through your own iCloud account, so they come back if you reinstall and match across your devices. We can't see them.

Camera, photos and files

Tally uses the camera only when you tap to scan a receipt. It only opens photos, PDFs and files that you choose to import or share with it.

Face ID

If you turn on the app lock, Face ID or Touch ID is handled entirely by iOS. Tally never receives your face or fingerprint data.

iPhone search

Tally can add your receipts to iPhone search (Spotlight) so you can find them quickly. This index is kept on your iPhone by iOS and isn't shared with us.

Diagnostics reports

Settings › Diagnostics shows information to help fix problems. It stays on your iPhone unless you choose Send Report and send it yourself. A report includes your property and folder names and file names from any recent errors, but not receipt pictures or amounts. If you send one to us, we use it only to help you and delete it when the issue is resolved.

Emails to us

If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We don't add you to any mailing list or share your details.

Children

Tally is not directed at children. There are no advertising, analytics or app-account features for collecting children’s information.

Your rights

We do not receive your app content through normal on-device processing. If you contact us or send a report, we may hold the information you provide to help with your request. If you've emailed us and want to see or delete that correspondence, contact us at support@riverinvest.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Tally handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. We do not add website analytics, advertising trackers or third-party fonts. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of website information.
