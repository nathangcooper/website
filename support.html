Useful answers

Common questions

Where are my receipts saved?+

In the folder you chose for each property in your iCloud Drive. Receipts without a property folder go to Receipt River's own folder, on your iPhone (Files › On My iPhone › Receipt River), or in iCloud Drive if Settings › Keep in iCloud Drive is on.

Will I lose my receipts if I delete the app?+

Receipts in iCloud Drive are kept. Receipts kept only on your iPhone are deleted along with the app, so we recommend turning on Settings › Keep in iCloud Drive, or using Settings › Back Up Receipts.

The details were read wrongly. What can I do?+

Check and correct the details before saving. Reading is most accurate on iPhones with Apple Intelligence; on other iPhones, scanning in good light with the receipt flat helps. Corrections to a shop's name can be remembered for next time.

How do I get my receipts into a spreadsheet?+

Use Export in the app to create a spreadsheet file (CSV) for any properties and dates. The details are also embedded in each receipt picture for compatible tools. CSV is the straightforward option for a spreadsheet.

How do I move to a new iPhone?+

Sign in with the same Apple Account to access your iCloud Drive files. Settings may sync through iCloud; you may need to select your folders again. Back up or export files kept only on the old phone before changing devices.

Can I share a property folder with someone else?+

Yes. Share the folder from the Files app, and each person adds it in Receipt River. Their receipts appear for everyone with access.

Does Receipt River give tax advice?+

No. It's a record-keeping tool. Check your figures with your accountant before submitting a VAT return.

Does Receipt River collect my data?+

There is no app account, advertising or developer analytics. You control whether to file, share or send a support report. Read the Receipt River privacy policy for the details, including iCloud and optional support emails.
