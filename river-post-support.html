Common questions

What does River Post save?

Each letter is saved as an ordinary PDF. Inside that file River Post stores the important details: who it's from, the date, the reference, any amount due, what you need to do and by when, a short summary, the letter's text and any extra fields you add. The letter and its details always travel together as one file.

Where are my letters saved?

In the folders you add in Settings, in iCloud Drive or anywhere in the Files app. Letters without a folder go to River Post's own folder, on your iPhone (Files › On My iPhone › Post), or in iCloud Drive if Settings › Keep in iCloud Drive is on.

Will I lose my letters if I delete the app?

Letters in iCloud Drive are kept. Letters kept only on your iPhone are deleted along with the app, so we recommend turning on Settings › Keep in iCloud Drive, or using More › Back Up Letters to copy them to a drive you choose.

Can I scan several letters at once?

Yes. Scan a pile in one go: River Post works out where each letter starts, leaves out blank pages, and you check each letter in turn. You can also share letters to River Post from Mail, Photos or Files.

The details were read wrongly. What can I do?

Check and correct the details before saving. Reading is most accurate on iPhones with Apple Intelligence; on other iPhones, scanning in good light with the letter flat helps. A corrected sender's name can be remembered for next time, and Health › Similar sender names tidies up a sender spelt in different ways.

How do reminders work?

Swipe right on a letter, or open it and tap Remind Me. River Post reminds you on the day you choose, and ticking the reminder off marks the letter's action as done. Reminders are kept in the app on your iPhone, so people sharing a folder with you don't see yours.

How do I get my letters into a spreadsheet?

Use Export in More to create a spreadsheet file (CSV) for any folders, categories and dates, including a UK tax year. The details are also stored inside each PDF, so other tools can read them straight from the folder.

Can I share a letter without my personal details?

Yes. Open the letter and tap Redact & Share. River Post covers account numbers, addresses, names and other personal details, which you can change before sharing. The shared copy is a picture of each page, with nothing hidden underneath.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account. Your settings and iCloud Drive letters come across automatically. You may need to choose each folder again in Settings.

Can I share a folder with my household?

Yes. Share the folder from the Files app, and each person adds it in River Post. Settings › Share Settings sends your categories, rules and names, so everyone files letters the same way.

Does River Post collect my data?

No. There's no account, no tracking and no server. See our privacy policy.
