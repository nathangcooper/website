Before you start

Good to know.

AI models need an initial download and storage space. Transcription and note writing run while Notali is open and pause when you leave. Features and summary detail depend on the model and available memory. AI output can be wrong; check important details.

Notali cannot record the microphone of an active phone, FaceTime or other call on the same iPhone. You can import a call recording you already have. Make sure everyone involved knows when you are recording.
