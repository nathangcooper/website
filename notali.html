Notali

Record a meeting, lecture or call and your iPhone transcribes it, works out who was speaking and writes up the notes. Every point links back to the moment it was said. Nothing is uploaded.

Notes that show their working

Each point in your notes is marked as said outright, the AI's reading, or still open, and links to the lines it came from. Tap one to read and hear exactly what was said.

What it does

Record or import

Record on your iPhone, or bring in recordings from Voice Memos, Files and other apps. Recording carries on with the screen locked.

Transcribed on your iPhone

Fast transcription on the iPhone's Neural Engine, with speakers told apart. Unsure words are underlined and corrected from context, and every change can be undone.

Written up for you

An overview, key points, risks and decisions, chapters for long recordings, and actions with owners and due dates gathered across all your recordings.

Ask your recordings

Search every transcript, or ask a question such as "What did we agree in the last three meetings?" and get an answer with links to where it was said.

Filed where you want

File notes and recordings into your own folders in Files or iCloud Drive. Export as PDF, Markdown, subtitles or a version made for AI assistants.

Private by design

No account, no adverts, no tracking. Your recordings never leave your iPhone unless you share or file them. Lock the app with Face ID.

Good to know

The AI models are downloaded once in the app, from about 1 GB to 3 GB depending on the quality you choose, then Notali works offline. Processing runs while the app is open; leaving the app pauses it and it carries on when you come back.

Notali works best in English and other European languages. AI notes can contain mistakes, so check anything important against the recording.
