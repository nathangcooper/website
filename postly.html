The useful details,
taken care of.

01

A letter you can find again

Letters are saved as searchable PDFs, with their details embedded in the same file.

02

From a pile to a filing system

Scan several letters and review where each one starts. Add folders for your household, properties or projects.

03

The next step, made visible

Keep amounts due, deadlines and reminders alongside the letter. Check extracted dates before relying on them.

04

Search the words, not the drawer

Search letter text, connect letters to people and places, and ask questions on supported devices.

05

Useful beyond the app

Export details as a CSV, back up your letters to a location you choose, or share a redacted copy.

06

Your documents, your storage

Choose folders in Files or iCloud Drive. The app uses on-device text recognition and, where available, Apple Intelligence.

Good to know.

Postly is a record-keeping tool. Review dates, amounts and suggested actions against the original letter. Its features do not replace legal or financial advice.
