Made for everyday use

The useful details,
taken care of.

01

One receipt, one useful file

Each receipt is a JPEG with its details embedded in the file. The image and its record stay together.

02

A home for every property

Keep receipts in folders for your properties or projects. Set rules for repeat merchants to reduce the filing.

03

A second look at the figures

Review missing details, possible duplicates and VAT figures that may not add up before saving.

04

Ready for your records

Filter spending by property, date or VAT quarter, then export a CSV for your spreadsheet or accountant.

05

Bring your receipts with you

Scan a batch or import from Mail, Photos and Files. Choose local storage or your own iCloud Drive.

06

Read on your iPhone

Text recognition runs on the device. Apple Intelligence can help read the details on supported iPhones.

Before you start

Good to know.

Receipt River is free and requires no app account. Check extracted details before saving. It is a record-keeping tool, not a source of tax advice.
