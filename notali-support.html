Useful answers

Common questions

Why does the summary look different on another iPhone?+

Notali adapts to the selected model and available memory. The smallest notes model writes a Basic Summary as short paragraphs without verified citations. Larger supported models can write structured notes with source links. Check important points against the transcript and recording.

Why does recording say the microphone is busy?+

A phone, FaceTime or other call can prevent Notali from starting a recording. End the call or stop another audio recording, then try again. Notali cannot record an active call on the same iPhone; import an existing recording instead.

Does anything get uploaded?+

Transcription, speaker identification, note writing and Ask processing run on your iPhone. Recordings are not sent to us for processing. If you choose to file or share a recording, your selected storage or sharing service handles that copy. AI models are downloaded during setup and may need later updates.

Writing notes failed. Do I need to transcribe everything again?+

Usually not. Use Try Again on the failed summary to reuse valid saved progress. Write Again deliberately starts a fresh summary. Use full reprocessing only when you want to replace the transcription as well.

Why is there a large download when I start?+

The AI models run on your iPhone, so they must be downloaded and stored on it. The space needed depends on your selected models; check Settings › AI Models for the current choices. Once the required models are available, transcription and note writing can work offline.

Why does processing stop when I leave the app?+

Transcribing and writing notes take a lot of the iPhone's power, and iOS stops apps doing heavy work in the background. Notali pauses when you leave and carries on from where it stopped when you come back. Recording itself keeps going in the background.

A name or word keeps coming out wrong. What can I do?+

Add words in Settings › Vocabulary, or add an automatic spelling fix. Vocabulary listening depends on the selected transcription model and whether vocabulary checking is enabled. In the Transcript tab, use Edit transcript to correct a line by hand.

How do I name the speakers?+

Open a note, tap the speakers count under its title, and give each one a name. You can play a sample of each voice to tell them apart. Once named, Notali recognises the same voice in later recordings.

Which languages does it support?+

The default transcription works with English and 24 other European languages. For other languages, such as Japanese or Chinese, choose Best in Settings › AI Models.

Where are my notes kept?+

On your iPhone, inside Notali. You can file any note into a folder you choose in Files or iCloud Drive, which also saves its recording and a readable copy there.

Will I lose my notes if I delete the app?+

Notes kept only in Notali are deleted with the app. Notes you've filed into a folder in Files or iCloud Drive stay there. Export or file anything you want to keep first.

Can I redo the notes or the transcript?+

Yes. Open the note, tap the menu, and choose Process Again: Notes Only rewrites the title, actions, chapters and notes, and Everything transcribes the recording again too.

Does Notali collect my data?+

There is no app account, advertising or developer analytics. You control whether to file, share or send a support report. Read the Notali privacy policy for the details, including iCloud and optional support emails.
