River Post

Scan a letter and your iPhone reads who it's from, any amount due and what you need to do by when. Check it, choose the folder and save. Then the paper can go. Free, private, no account.

The details travel with the letter

Each letter is saved as an ordinary, searchable PDF with its details stored inside the file. The pages and the details are never separated, so spreadsheets, document tools and AI assistants can read them straight from the file.

What it does

Scan a pile at once

Scan your post in one go and River Post works out where each letter starts. Or share PDFs and photos from Mail, Photos and Files.

Read on your iPhone

Sender, date, reference, amount due, deadline and a short summary are read on the device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Nothing missed

Reminders for payments, deadlines and renewals, and a Coming Up box with what's due next and how much you have to pay.

Find any letter

Search every word of every letter, link letters to the people and properties they're about, and ask questions such as "How much was the water bill last year?"

Kept in good order

Health finds missing details, possible duplicates and senders spelt two ways. Export a spreadsheet, and back up every letter to a drive you choose.

Private by design

No account, no adverts, no tracking. Your letters stay on your iPhone or in your own iCloud Drive. Hide account numbers before you share a copy.

Good to know

Reading is most accurate on iPhones with Apple Intelligence. On other iPhones, River Post uses Apple's built-in text recognition, so check the details before saving.

River Post is a record-keeping tool and doesn't give legal or financial advice.
