Privacy, explained.

Your content is processed on your iPhone. No app account, advertising or developer analytics.

Privacy policy · Updated 3 November 2026

The essentials

We do not receive your documents for processing. Files stay on your iPhone or in storage you choose. If you email us or share a support report, we receive the information you send. Website hosting and your chosen storage services have their own privacy practices.

Who we are

Notali is made by River Investment Partners LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in England and Wales (number OC457091), registered office Flat 23, Russell Chambers, London WC1A 2JU. You can contact us at support@riverinvest.co.uk.

What Notali collects

Notali has no app account system, advertising, tracking or developer-run analytics. Your documents are not uploaded to us for processing. Optional support emails, shared reports and storage services you choose are described below.

How your recordings are processed

Transcription, speaker identification, note-writing and answers to your questions are all done by AI models running on your iPhone. Your recordings, transcripts and notes are not uploaded for processing. If you choose Apple Intelligence as the notes engine, Notali uses only its on-device model.

Internet connections

Notali downloads AI models from Apple’s App Store servers or Hugging Face. These downloads do not include your recordings, transcripts or notes. The provider may receive technical connection information, such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. Filing, sharing and support emails use the services you choose.

Where your notes are kept

Recordings, transcripts and notes are kept on your iPhone, inside Notali. If you file a note into a folder you choose, in the Files app or in iCloud Drive, its recording and a copy of the note are saved there. Files in iCloud Drive or other storage services are kept by that service under your own account and its privacy policy. We have no access to them.

Voices

When you name a speaker, Notali saves a voice profile on your iPhone so it can recognise that voice in later recordings. Voice profiles never leave your iPhone, and you can remove them in Settings › Known Voices.

Microphone, files and Face ID

Notali uses the microphone only while you record. It only opens recordings you choose to import or share with it, and the folders you choose for filing. If you turn on Require Face ID, Face ID is handled entirely by iOS and Notali never receives your face data.

Diagnostics reports

Settings › Diagnostics keeps a log of what the app did, to help fix problems. It stays on your iPhone unless you choose to share it yourself. It may include note titles and error messages, but not your recordings or transcripts. If you send one to us, we use it only to help you and delete it when the issue is resolved.

Emails to us

If you email us for support or feedback, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We don't add you to any mailing list or share your details.

Children

Notali is not directed at children. There are no advertising, analytics or app-account features for collecting children’s information.

Your rights

We do not receive your app content through normal on-device processing. If you contact us or send a report, we may hold the information you provide to help with your request. If you've emailed us and want to see or delete that correspondence, contact us at support@riverinvest.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Notali handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. We do not add website analytics, advertising trackers or third-party fonts. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of website information.
