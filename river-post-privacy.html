Privacy Policy

River Post for iPhone · Last updated 18 October 2026

In short: River Post doesn't collect any personal data. There's no account, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking, and we have no server. Your letters stay on your iPhone or in your own iCloud Drive, and we can't see them.

Who we are

River Post is made by River Investment Partners LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in England and Wales (number OC457091), registered office Flat 23, Russell Chambers, London WC1A 2JU. You can contact us at support@riverinvest.co.uk.

What River Post collects

Nothing. River Post doesn't send any information to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it doesn't make network connections of its own.

How your letters are read

When you scan or import a letter, the text is read on your iPhone using Apple's built-in text recognition and, where available, Apple Intelligence running on the device. Questions you ask about your letters are answered on the device in the same way. Your letters and their details are not uploaded for reading.

Where your letters are kept

Letters are saved as PDF files in places you choose:

  • on your iPhone, in River Post's own folder (shown in the Files app), or
  • in your iCloud Drive, in River Post's folder or in folders you add in Settings.

Files in iCloud Drive are stored by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple's own privacy policy. We have no access to them. If you share a folder with someone else, they can see the letters in it. Backups you make go only to the place you choose.

Settings and reminders

Your River Post settings, such as your name, folders, categories, rules, people and places, and reminders, are kept on your iPhone and synced through your own iCloud account, so they come back if you reinstall and match across your devices. We can't see them.

Notifications and calendar

River Post asks to send notifications only when you set your first reminder, and reminders are scheduled by iOS on your iPhone. Add to Calendar opens Apple's own event screen, so River Post doesn't need access to your calendars and can't read them.

Camera, photos and files

River Post uses the camera only when you tap to scan a letter. It only opens photos, PDFs and files that you choose to import or share with it, and the folders you add.

Face ID

If you turn on the app lock, Face ID or Touch ID is handled entirely by iOS. River Post never receives your face or fingerprint data.

iPhone search

River Post can add your letters to iPhone search (Spotlight) so you can find them quickly. This index is kept on your iPhone by iOS, isn't shared with us, and isn't made while the app lock is on.

Diagnostics reports

Settings › Diagnostics shows information to help fix problems. It stays on your iPhone unless you choose Send Report and send it yourself. A report includes your folder names and file names from any recent errors, but not your letters or their details. If you send one to us, we use it only to help you and delete it when the issue is resolved.

Emails to us

If you email us for support or feedback, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We don't add you to any mailing list or share your details.

Children

River Post isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because River Post doesn't collect personal data, we hold none about you from using the app. If you've emailed us and want to see or delete that correspondence, contact us at support@riverinvest.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how River Post handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
